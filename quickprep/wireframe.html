Overview

Purpose

Quick Prep is a simple and responsive recipe site designed to help students and busy individuals find and save quick, healthy, and budget friendly meals. It aims to make cooking feel easy and fun

Audience

This site is for college students, young adults, and beginner cooks who need easy meals without complicated ingredients or hours of cooking time. Ideal for people on a budget or new to meal planning

Dynamic elements

JavaScript will power interactive recipe filters, have a search bar, do a light/dark mode, and a dynamic recipe rating display using stars. Also a recipie of the day feature that changes daily.
